# Sound notes

Sound can shape the story from the beginning. Keep narration, native dialogue,
score, atmosphere, and effects separately editable. The H3 picture adapter strips
its generated audio; that is the adapter's contract, not a judgment about every
model or every possible production.

## Music

Follow emotional movement with contrasting orchestration, rhythm, register,
texture, density, space, and silence. Awe, dread, grief, hope, devotion, intimacy,
scale, and kinetic energy are useful lenses when they help; no film needs to fill
out all eight. Selective Indian colour can coexist with modern sounds without
repeating the same drone/flute/orchestral shape for every scene.

Give the score meaningful presence, including speech-free moments. Make room for
voices deliberately instead of stacking attenuation until the music disappears.
Action peaks and quiet scenes can have different balances. Judge the whole scene.

Musical edits often preserve a cue better than stretching it to an unrelated
length. Listen to the returned composition and use its actual duration. Preserve
starts, endings, and exact prompts when they are useful for later revisions.

## Effects

Short concrete material/action prompts have produced more variety than appending
"epic bass, thunder, long reverb" to everything. Try different acoustic mechanisms.
Story reasoning can stay in the brief while the source prompt stays simple.

For fantasy, lightning, pressure, explosions, wordless cries, and vast resonance
can express scale or transformation without literal weather. Contrast them with
close detail, air, motion, evolving textures, and silence. Vary attack, body,
movement, and decay through selective layers, pitch/speed changes, and edits.

A stable middle section can extend a texture without repeating its attack. Keep
raw originals and treatment parameters. Speech-like artifacts or competing music
may be unwanted in an effect; listen to what the source actually contributes.
Self-hosted text-to-audio is an available route, not a compulsory creative choice.

## Audition and refine

Make a mix to hear it. Compare raw sounds and a few labelled scene versions when
that answers a real question; do not build an approval ceremony around every cue.
Waveforms and loudness measurements help diagnosis but cannot establish taste.
Record listening limits honestly and preserve useful observations for reuse.

Inspect the combined gain of all processing stages and measure the encoded
result. Preserve complete speech and native sync. A sound-only revision can
reuse accepted picture and HD caches rather than regenerate the film.
